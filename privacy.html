Privacy

Privacy on this website

Last updated 14 September 2026

The short version

This website does not collect personal information. There are no forms, no accounts, no newsletter sign-up, no analytics, no advertising trackers, and it sets no cookies of its own.

What loads from other companies

To display the pages, your browser fetches typefaces, icons and styling from these services. Each receives your IP address and basic browser information as part of that request, which is how the web works for any hosted file:

  • Google Fonts — typefaces (Google privacy policy)
  • Cloudflare cdnjs — icons (Cloudflare privacy policy)
  • Tailwind CDN — page styling

The homepage also embeds a Google map showing the salon. Google may set cookies and collect usage data through that embed under its own policy. If you would rather not load it, the address is written out in text beside the map.

Booking an appointment

The booking buttons open Square Appointments on square.site, a separate website. Anything you enter there — your name, contact details and any payment information — is collected and held by Square under Square's privacy policy, not by this website. You can also book by phone instead.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub records requests to its servers, including IP addresses, to operate and secure the service — see the GitHub privacy statement.

In the salon

This page covers the website only. Appointment records and anything you share with us in person or over the phone are handled by the salon directly. Ask us any time and we'll explain what we hold.
